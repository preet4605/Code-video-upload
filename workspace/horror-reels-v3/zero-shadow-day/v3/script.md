# Zero Shadow Day v3: script

One rule, one chain: one minute a year nobody has a shadow → Dadi: whoever's hand you hold in that minute takes your shadow → the teacher forces hands → the empty place is drowned Raju's → shadows vanish → a cold hand takes his → it's Raju → "your shadow is mine now" → minute ends, he lets go, his shadow stays on Raju's feet → Mummy hugs Raju by his name → now he waits every year to take someone's hand.

## What changed from v2 and why
- v2 asked the viewer to hold four ideas (no-shadow minute, don't hold hands, dead and living look alike, only one shadow returns). v3 has one rule, said in plain words in lines 2 and 3, and the twist is that rule happening.
- Raju is introduced before he appears (the empty place in the ring), so "it was Raju" lands.
- The whisper now says what is happening ("your shadow is mine now") instead of restating the rule.
- The ending is complete: he becomes the cold hand. No question, no cliffhanger.
- Shorter lines (median ~11 words) and a clock time (12:07).
