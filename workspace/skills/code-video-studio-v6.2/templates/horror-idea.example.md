# IDEA (pitch, not a script): "Ek baar"

## Logline
A boy obeys his nani's night rule and ignores a single call from the kitchen; his sleeping mother answers it instead, and at dawn the call comes back.

## Myth / Rule
Nani's saying (invented family rule, presented as her saying): people call twice; whoever calls only once is not people. Never answer a once-call at night.

## Beats
1. Hook: state the rule in one line over a moonlit bedroom.
2. Nani and the rule (diya, raised finger).
3. 3:07. The kitchen calls "beta", once, in mother's voice. She is asleep beside him.
4. He stays silent. Mother mumbles "haan?" in her sleep: she has answered.
5. She walks to the kitchen; a light strip under the door shows two feet, then goes out. She never returns.
6. Morning: two warm cups on the counter. The call comes again. Once.

## The scare
The sleeping mother answering, then the light strip going out. Sound: slippers, a door, then silence.

## Ending
Last line echoes the hook: the voice calls once more.
