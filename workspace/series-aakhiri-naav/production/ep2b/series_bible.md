# Series bible: आख़िरी नाव (production extract)

## Characters
- narrator: the older brother, speaks as मैं, never named
- छोटा: the younger brother, missing for three nights, never named and never seen
- माँ: the mother (voice only, from ep4b)

## Places
- झील: a deep black lake in December
- नाव: a wooden boat with a lit four-pane cabin window
- किनारा: the mud shore below the village

## Rules
- नाव की आवाज़ का जवाब मत दो (village saying; invented story rule, not a real belief)
- अकेले झील पार मत करना (set in ep3)
- सुबह से पहले नाव को हाथ मत लगाओ (set in ep4b)

## Parts
- Part 1: the boat comes back empty and dry with the dry red jacket of the missing brother; vote A wade in, B stay.
- Part 2: branch A (ep2a) he wades in, answers the voice, a small hand pushes him out; branch B (ep2b) he stays silent, a walker comes under the water, the lake answers in his voice and a wave pushes him out. Both rejoin at ep3.

## Canon
- The narrator has called for his brother on the shore for three nights ("छोटे, मैं यहीं हूँ").
- The lake only returns what is said at it, in the voice that said it.
- The water is warm near the boat; the cabin murmurs "हाँ" in many voices.
- The voice that calls the narrator is his own voice, raised and softened, which he almost recognises.
- A small wet hand pushes him out of the water.
- The walker is under the water, upside down, with the brother's left lean.
- The lake can answer in the narrator's voice even when he stays silent.
- The walker is under the water and inverted; its face looks up at him from beneath the surface.
- Many small hands shove him out of the water.

## Open threads
- Whose small hand pushed him out?
- Why does the lake have his voice?
