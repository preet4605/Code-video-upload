# आख़िरी नाव — Series Bible v2

Replaces the v1 `SERIES.md`. Episode 1 is aired and fixed (`ep1_as_aired.md`). Everything after it is rewritten here. Scripts and shot lists live in `episodes/`.

**Tagline (v2):** जो पुकारता है, उसके लिए आख़िरी नाव लौटती है।
**Genre:** Hindi horror, first person, 9:16, Devanagari captions. **FICTION:** the lake, the boat, the voices and the rules are story only.

## 0. What changed from v1, and why

| v1 | v2 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Each episode taught a cold-water fact (F1–F4, U1) | Facts survive only as **body details** (the gasp, the shivering that stops). No lectures, no myth-busting lines | The survival frame is from a different series (`survival-series`). Inside horror it flattens dread. |
| The boat is a haunted object | The boat is **the lake's answer to the narrator's own call** (ep5) | A reason that re-reads ep1: it comes back because *he* asks it to. |
| The voice is a kind stranger | The voice is **his own VO, raised and softened** ("almost recognised") | Makes the horror personal, and the audio pays it off in ep5. |
| Rule one: kept costs a wave | Rule one kept **does not save him**; the lake answers in his voice | A rule you can obey perfectly and still lose is scarier than a rule you break. |
| Rule two: boat has a warm cabin | The cabin is **his mother's kitchen, built wrong**; the "companion" is his reflection | Original set piece, ties to the rule's loophole. |
| Rule three set in ep4b by "the village" | Set by **his mother through a locked door** | Gives ep4b a human, emotional scene. |
| Bottle in the pack, alcohol myth | Cut | A myth beat that does nothing for dread. |
| Ask text per episode in a card | Same ask, exact, but options now carry *different dread* | The V6.1 gate requires an earned fork (`branches.md`). |

## 1. The truth (internal; never stated in full in one place)

1. The lake keeps calls. It cannot speak first. It can only **return** what is said at it, in the voice that said it.
2. Three nights ago the narrator's younger brother vanished at the lake. Since then the narrator has stood on the shore every night shouting "छोटे, लौट आ" and "छोटे, मैं यहीं हूँ".
3. The lake **answers** every call. It answered the first two nights with a dark empty boat. Tonight it answers with a lit boat carrying the brother's jacket, because it has learned from three nights what he wants.
4. Everything he meets is his own call returned: the voice (his VO, raised and softened), the hand-sized warmth, the cabin that copies his memories, the walker with the left lean, the answer "हाँ, मैं यहीं हूँ" (his own call words).
5. Rule one is the village's defence: **never answer an echo**, because an answered echo becomes a bond. The village learned this because every grieving family has stood on that shore.
6. A small hand has been shoving him *out* of the lake each time he gets close. It is the brother (or the part of him the lake could not keep). It signals with the phone, steps in his footprints, ties the left-handed knot.
7. At dawn, with the first human sound, the lake's hold breaks. What comes out is a small wet hand, the brother's, answering in the narrator's voice because that is the only voice the lake owns. **Whether it is the brother or the echo is Story 2.**

## 4. Cast and canon

- **Narrator.** Older brother. Unnamed. Calm, plain speech, scared under it. First person.
- **The brother ("छोटा").** Never named, never seen alive in the present. Left-handed (ep3), steps in the narrator's footprints (ep4b, ep5), coconut hair oil (ep2a), cricket sticker on his phone (ep3), phone ringtone set by the narrator on his birthday (ep3). Lean to the left when he walks (ep2b). **Rule: the brother is only ever a jacket, a hand, a knot, a walk or a footprint. No face.**
- **The mother.** New in ep4b. Alive, locked in, voice only, whispering. She calls the narrator "तू". The father is not mentioned.
- **The village.** Up the hill. Windows dark; its silence is a defence. No one appears on screen.
- **The lake.** Black, still, glass-like. Not frozen. Warm only near the boat (ep2a). No depth number is spoken.
- **The boat.** Wooden, cabin with a four-pane window (as aired), lantern on the cabin's right, the red jacket on the bow seat. Its hull does not touch the water (a black gap, ep2a). Its cabin inside (ep4a) is his kitchen with wrong handles.
- **The voice.** The narrator's own VO, pitched up, softened, late. It calls his full name, which only his mother used. The name is never audible; see §10.
- **The walker (ep2b).** Under the surface, upside down, left lean. The face is a pale oval; no eyes.
- **The small hand.** Wet, cold. Shoves him out (ep2a, ep2b, ep3 print), holds the phone (ep3), ties the knot (ep5), takes his hand (ep5).
- **Time.** Dusk (ep1), the last light into night (ep2a, ep2b), deep night (ep3), the same night (ep4a, ep4b), dawn (ep5). December, clear, wind off the lake.
- **Props allowed:** the red jacket, the dead phone (his), the brother's phone (under the lake), the lantern, the knot, steel cups, the signboard. **No knife, no lighter, no bottle.**

## 7. Format and vote mechanics (kept from v1, mapped to the V6.1 gates)

- **Seven renders** serve five-episode paths: ep1 (aired), ep2a, ep2b, ep3, ep4a, ep4b, ep5.
- **Votes after ep1, ep3, ep5.** Ask line is exactly `कमेंट करो, A या B?`, the last spoken line, and also in the caption. Option text lives on the choice card and the caption.
- **`brief.format`:** ep1, ep3, ep5 = `interactive`. ep2a, ep2b, ep4a, ep4b = `serial` (they rejoin, they do not fork; V6.1's interactive gate would force a fork on every part). `series.next_hook` for the serial four is the cliff line named in each episode doc.
- **Recap line:** every part opens with a recap-hook of ≤ 8 s (`hook_is_recap: true`), so a new viewer can start anywhere.
- **Length:** V6.1's band is 60–90 s per part. All six scripts are 215–240 words and estimate to 81–90 s. See each doc's trim ladder; measure real TTS first.
- **Pinned comment:** ONE comment: count, both branch links, the tie rule. (V6.1 allows `pinned_comment` or `story_poll` as the vote channel; there is no Reels poll sticker.)
- **Tie rule:** the branch whose ask was posted first wins. Post 2a before 2b, 4a before 4b.
- **Window:** 24 h, comments read by hand. No keyword automation is assumed.
- **Safe zone:** x 60–900, y 270–1440 (V6.1 default). Keep action above y ≈ 1250. No full-frame strobe; every "lights out" is a fade to black, never white.
- **Pilot rule:** render one episode first and check it with sound on a phone. Recommended pilot: **ep2a**, because it fixes the look and the lake-voice chain every later episode reuses.

### 7a. STANDING RULE: choice-recap intro on every part (Love, 2026-11-25)

**Every episode opens with a choice recap of about 6-7 s, before its own hook.** It tells a new viewer what the last question was, shows both options, and says which path this video is. Never skip it, never replace it with only a caption.
- **Look:** the dusk boat of ep1 behind a heading pill `पिछली बार का सवाल`, then two cards `A नाव तक जाओ` / `B किनारे रुको` (the card text always comes from the previous vote's real options). The path this video follows lights up and gets the tag `अब यह रास्ता`; the other card dims and says `वह भी बना है`.
- **Voice (four lines, same narrator voice, no processing):** `पिछली बार सवाल था।` / `A, <option A>।` / `B, <option B>।` / `<A या B> चुनते तो यह होता।`
- **Honesty rule:** if the vote had real winners, `चुनते तो` may become `आपने ... चुना`. With zero votes (ep1) the wording stays `चुनते तो यह होता`; never claim the audience chose something they did not.
- **Every part now follows a vote** (full fork or light fork, see §7b), so the intro always lights the winning option of the vote that led here. Cards and wording come from that vote (for ep3: the ep2 question `A उसे छूकर देखो` / `B झील को पुकारो`).
- **Budget:** the intro is on top of the story. ep2a becomes 90.1 s and ep2b 94.4 s; the platform maximum for Reels in V6.1 is 180 s, only the serial 60-90 s soft band is exceeded. Trim body lines on new episodes so body + intro stays near 90 s.
- **Build:** `production/shared/tools/intro_scene.tpl.mjs` (set `__CHOICE__`), `intro_audio.py`, then stitch with ffmpeg concat (re-encode, limiter at -2.2 dB, check LUFS ~ -14.5 and true peak <= -1.5). Rendered with `render_and_lint.mjs --skip-story-lint` because the intro is not a story. Recipe is in `production/INTRO_RECIPE.md`.

### 7b. STANDING RULE: every part ENDS with an A/B question that picks the next video (Love, 2026-11-25)

**No episode may end without the ask.** Last spoken line is exactly `कमेंट करो, A या B?` and the on-screen cards stay up. I broke this rule on the first pass by leaving ep2a/ep2b as "serial" parts with no question; fixed on 2026-11-25. Do not repeat.
- **Honesty test:** the answer must visibly change the next video. A question whose answer is ignored is a fake vote and is not allowed.
- **Full fork** (two complete next episodes): after ep1 (ep2a / ep2b), after ep3 (ep4a / ep4b), after ep5 (Story 2).
- **Light fork** (the next episode exists once; only its first 12-15 s differ, then both openers rejoin the same body): after ep2a/ep2b -> `ep3` opener A or B; after ep4a/ep4b -> `ep5` opener A or B (question to be written when ep4 is made; it must rhyme with ep5's dock/knot). Cost: two short openers per light fork, not a second episode. Reason: a full fork after every part doubles the renders each time.
- **ep2a and ep2b ask the same question**, because they merge: `अब तुम बताओ, मेरे कंधे पर कुछ गीला है। A, उसे छूकर देखो। B, झील को पुकारो। कमेंट करो, A या B?` Count the comments of BOTH reels together; tie -> A (ep2a was posted first). Pinned comment on each reel: `A = उसे छूकर देखो · B = झील को पुकारो, 24 ghante`.
- **ep3 openers (sketch; real lines written at ep3 production):** A: he touches the print; four short fingers and a thumb, still wet. B: he calls "छोटे?" low across the water and his own voice answers "हाँ, मैं यहीं हूँ"; ep3 L2 then changes "बजी" to a green light first. Both keep the rejoin invariants (handprint on the right shoulder blade, boat beside him) and run into the same L2 phone scene. Rule one stays honest: B breaks it, A keeps it.
- **Outro look (about 9.3 s):** night lake, dark boat, heading pill `अब तुम बताओ`, cards A/B appearing with their lines, then cards alternate (0.7 s each, no flash faster than that) with a pill `कमेंट में A या B लिखो`. Tool: `production/shared/tools/` outro template = `production/outro2/scene.mjs`.
- **Stitch order:** recap intro (§7a) + episode + outro in ONE re-encode (see `production/INTRO_RECIPE.md`), limiter -2.2 dB, check ~-14.5 LUFS and peak <= -2 dBFS. Posting files are `production/final/*_final.mp4`.
- **Length cost:** ep2a 99.3 s, ep2b 103.7 s with both add-ons (platform maximum is 180 s; the 60-90 s serial band is exceeded). On later episodes trim the body so intro + body + outro stays near 90-95 s.

## 8. Language rules (unchanged unless noted)

- Friend test: every spoken line works said aloud to a friend. Full sentences with connectors.
- No spoken fragment of 2–4 words as a rhetorical beat. (Allowed: ordinary replies like `ठीक हूँ, भैया।` and `तू कौन है?`; these are character lines.)
- Numbers spelled in words. None appear in these scripts as digits.
- Latin letters only in the ask and the choice-recap intro (§7a): A and B.
- Banned words (V6.1): अचानक, खौफनाक, भयानक, डरावना, रहस्यमय. TTS traps: निगल, बँटती, विज्ञान. Checked in all six scripts (my own checks, not the V6.1 gate; see §13).
- One direct fear statement per episode at most. The six v2 scripts use none; they show objects instead.
- Loop: the last payoff line echoes a word from the hook (checked by eye, listed in each doc's `callback`).

## 9. Visual language

- **World** (3D, `lib/scene3d.mjs`): a mud shore, a black glass lake, a ridge on the right, the village up the hill, the boat. Match ep1's boat silhouette and props so the move from flat vector to 3D reads as the camera coming closer.
- **Colour script.** Ep1 dusk orange → night blue (ep2a/2b) → cold blue plus one green (ep3) → amber only inside the cabin (ep4a) → cold gravel road and one warm window (ep4b) → ash grey, then real sun, the only warm natural light in the series (ep5).
- **Warm light lies.** Cabin amber and lamp 2700 K are the lake's offers. Cold blue is truth. The sun at the end is the first light with no motive behind it.
- **Reflection and water.** The lake is a surface that *answers*: rings from his voice (ep5), the reflection that rows (ep4a), the walker underneath (ep2b).
- **Every shot moves.** A handheld tremor that grows toward the end of each episode. Pre-scare shots are the *stillest* in the episode (ep2a S8, ep4a S7).

## 10. Sound language (the series' signature)

- **The lake chain.** Every lake voice is **the narrator's own TTS line** (same speaker as the narration), processed: pitch +2 semitones for the kind voice, +5 for the brother-ish answer (ep4a); formant slightly up; pre-delay 0.7 s; a low-passed tail at ~3.5 kHz; tiny 40 ms drop-outs at word joins (the splices of a voice assembled from his calls).
- **The name** (`name_in_water`). His full name is never intelligible. It is his own voice, reversed and under water, with a gurgle. Captions show nothing for it.
- **The mother** (ep4b). A separate female voice, dry, whispered, filtered as if through a door (low-pass ~2 kHz plus a wood resonance).
- **Silence before the scare.** Each episode has one `[SILENCE dur=1.0]` and a ≥ 20 dB pre-scare drop. The scare is a designed stinger: a sub drop and a glass crack, plus a chromatic spike. No white flash.
- **Ep5's trick.** The final `[CHARACTER]` line is the same TTS voice with **no processing at all**. The audience hears that the kind voice was the narrator's. This is the whole twist in one effect, so do not mix it down.
- **Sound motifs.** A water-held murmur of "हाँ" (ep2a). A slow breath in the hull. The wet *pat* of small steps. An even tick of steps (ep2b). The ringtone under water (ep3). A clock slightly off tempo (ep4a).
- **Caption styling** (Devanagari): narrator white with the V6.1 highlight word. Lake voice in pale blue with a 0.7 s ghost duplicate. Mother in warm amber.

## 11. Fiction notice, facts and guards

- **FICTION:** the boat, the lake's voice, the walker, the cabin, the small hand, the rules, the village, the brother and the narrator. No episode presents any of it as real, safety advice, or "a true story".
- **Real facts used, as body details only:**
  - Cold-water entry forces an involuntary gasp (ep2a L2). **VERIFIED** in v1 (Wikipedia "Cold shock response").
  - Shivering fades as hypothermia deepens, and a person may undress while still very cold (ep3 L6). Shivering stopping is **VERIFIED** (v1 ledger F3). "Paradoxical undressing" was checked in a web search today: it is described in the clinical literature; its mechanism is unconfirmed. The script shows it as his behaviour and never claims the cause.
  - People often do not recognise recordings of their own voice. Not spoken as a fact; it is the reason "almost recognised" works. **UNVERIFIED**, not fetched.
- **Guards:** never tell a viewer to enter cold water, go to a boat alone, or drink to warm up. No episode says "यह सच्ची कहानी है".

## 12. Story 2 seeds (ideas, not scripts; the winner of the ep5 vote chooses)

- **A (turn and look):** the face is the brother's, but its mouth moves a half-second *after* the sound, like the lips of the walker in ep2b. The thing wants to be seen exactly once, and then it needs him to say its name, which he has never said aloud in the series.
- **B (walk it home):** he takes the hand up the hill to his mother's door. She cannot tell the two silent figures from the thing behind them. The rule becomes: whoever speaks first is the lake. A two-handed standoff at a locked door.
- The brother's name is held back for Story 2 on purpose.

## 13. Honest risks (for the producer)

1. **Ep1's numbers.** 1,302 views, 0 comments, 14.2 s average watch out of 55.5 s (read 2026-11-25). Most people leave in the first quarter. The new episodes cannot fix ep1. Each one opens on a ≤ 8 s recap and a fresh image so they work cold.
2. **A vote nobody votes in.** With 0 comments, the creator is the real tiebreaker. The structure still makes sense, because both branches are pre-produced and publish the same day.
3. **Length.** The estimate uses 2.7 words/s from ep1. Real TTS may differ ±10%. Use the trim ladders. Do not speed the VO up (V6.1: tempo 0.93–1.0).
4. **Render difficulty.** The three hardest shots: ep2b S6 (the walker flips under the surface), ep4a S10 (the reflection that rows), ep5 S6 (ripples as the waveform of his voice). Each doc names a fallback.
5. **Style mismatch.** Ep1 is flat vector; the new episodes ask for a 3D look. Keep the same boat and palette so it reads as continuity.
6. **Gates not run.** I checked each script for digits, Latin letters, banned words, line count, one silence and one scare marker. I did **not** run `script_gate.mjs`, `story_lint` or `render_and_lint`; they need `brief.json`, `shotlist.json`, `branches.md` and `branch_tree.json` for each episode. The story-gate blocks in each doc map to those fields one to one, so the producing thread can generate them directly.
7. **Voice casting.** A second female voice (the mother) is new. Audition per `voice_audition.mjs`.
